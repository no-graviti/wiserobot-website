How Much Does Business Automation Cost in the UK?

6 min read · Pricing

One of the most common questions we get asked is simply: what does this actually cost? Pricing in this space varies a lot depending on who you ask, so here's a realistic, honest breakdown.

The two parts of the cost: setup and ongoing support

Most automation work is priced in two parts. A one-off setup fee covers the design, building, and testing of the automation. A smaller monthly support fee covers ongoing monitoring and fixes — automations need occasional attention as the tools they connect to update or change over time.

Typical price ranges

  • A single, simple automation (e.g. automatic lead follow-up connecting two tools): roughly £300–£800 one-off, plus £30–£60/month ongoing support.
  • A more involved automation with AI-powered steps (e.g. summarising and drafting responses to enquiries): roughly £800–£2,000 one-off, plus £50–£150/month.
  • A full process overhaul across multiple departments: typically £2,000+ and priced individually, since scope varies significantly business to business.

What affects the price

  • How many tools need connecting — each additional system (CRM, spreadsheet, email platform) adds some complexity.
  • Whether AI steps are involved — automations that need to "understand" content (summarising, drafting, classifying) take more design work than simple rule-based automations.
  • How messy the existing process is — a process with lots of exceptions and edge cases takes longer to automate reliably than a clean, consistent one.

Is it worth the cost?

A useful way to think about it: if a task currently takes an employee (or you) 5 hours a week, that's roughly 20+ hours a month. Even at a modest hourly value, automating that task typically pays for itself within a few months — and then keeps saving that time indefinitely afterward.
