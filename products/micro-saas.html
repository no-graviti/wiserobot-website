Common Questions

What is micro-SaaS?

A micro-SaaS is a small, tightly-focused piece of software that solves one specific problem well, rather than trying to be an all-in-one platform. It's built once and used by many businesses, so it's typically far cheaper than a custom-built automation.

How is this different from your automation service?

Our automation service is a custom build tailored exactly to your process. Micro-SaaS tools are off-the-shelf software you sign up for and use yourself — no custom setup, lower cost, but less tailored.
